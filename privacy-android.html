隐私政策（Android 版）

适用于 Google Play 上的「瞄准·AI舞蹈直拍」（包名 com.miaozhun.android）。iOS 版请见 iOS 隐私政策。

更新日期：2026年11月21日

运营者：KitCarry。联系邮箱：eggyrooch@gmail.com。

视频与设备端处理

你选择的视频用于舞者检测、跟踪、构图与导出，全部在你的手机上完成。瞄准 Android 版不申请网络权限，没有上传视频或任何数据到服务器的功能；无需注册账号；未接入广告、第三方统计或崩溃上报 SDK；不使用云端 AI 服务。识别与跟踪使用 App 内置模型在设备本地运行。

我们处理哪些数据

  • 你通过系统选择器选中的视频：复制到 App 私有目录作为项目源文件。
  • 由视频计算出的人体检测框、人体关键点、用于区分舞者的外观特征、跟踪轨迹与构图参数：保存在 App 私有目录的项目文件中，只用于跟随你选中的舞者，不用于识别人物真实身份，不创建人脸模板，不做人脸识别。
  • 你使用「人物保护」时：App 在设备本地分割画面中的人物，对选定范围（选中舞者、其他人物或全部人物）做模糊或硬遮，并在导出的视频里生效。你选择的模式、范围、强度，以及你用「手动补遮」添加的遮挡框位置、大小和生效时间段，保存在 App 私有目录的项目文件中，在你清除 App 数据或卸载 App 时一并删除；App 本身不会上传或发送这些数据（Android 系统云备份见下文「保存与分享」）。
  • App 不读取通讯录、位置、麦克风、相机或你的其他照片，也不申请相册读取权限。

保存与分享

只有你点击「保存到相册」时，成片才会写入系统相册（Movies/Miaozhun）。只有你点击「分享视频」时，成片才会交给你在系统分享菜单中选择的应用。系统相册、Android 系统云备份（如果你在手机上开启了该功能，系统可能备份 App 数据，包括项目文件）和你选择的分享服务分别受其各自政策约束。

保留与删除

项目数据保存在设备本地，直到你在系统设置中清除本 App 数据或卸载 App。卸载 App 会删除设备本地的全部项目数据；已保存到系统相册、已分享的视频，以及已被 Android 系统云备份的数据，需要在相册、对应服务或系统/Google 备份设置中管理或删除。

第三方共享

我们不收集、不出售、也不与任何第三方共享你的数据。Google Play 平台提供的安装量、崩溃统计等由 Google 按其政策处理，App 本身不发送任何数据。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计。

变更

功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策并修改更新日期。

Privacy Policy (Android)

Applies to "Miaozhun: AI Dance Fancam" on Google Play (package com.miaozhun.android). Updated: 21 November 2026. Operator: KitCarry. Contact: eggyrooch@gmail.com.

On-device processing. Videos you pick are used for dancer detection, tracking, framing and export, all on your phone. The Android app has no internet permission and cannot upload videos or any other data. It has no account, no ads, no third-party analytics or crash reporting, and no cloud AI.

Data processed. The video you pick in the system picker is copied into the app's private storage. Detection boxes, body keypoints, appearance features used to tell dancers apart, tracks and framing settings derived from it are stored locally in the project and used only to follow the dancer you chose, never to identify who a person is. No face templates are created and no face recognition is performed. The app does not access contacts, location, microphone, camera or your other photos, and requests no gallery read permission.

Person protection. If you use Person Protection, the app segments people in the video on your device and blurs or masks the people you choose (the selected dancer, other people, or everyone) in the exported video. Your chosen mode, scope and strength, and the position, size and time range of any manual cover boxes you add, are stored in the project in the app's private storage, are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it, and are never uploaded or sent by the app (see Saving and sharing for Android system cloud backup).

Saving and sharing. A video is written to your gallery (Movies/Miaozhun) only when you tap Save, and handed to another app only when you choose it in the system share sheet. The gallery, Android system cloud backup (if you have turned it on, the system may back up app data including project files) and the sharing service you pick are governed by their own policies.

Retention and deletion. Project data stays on your device until you clear the app's data in system settings or uninstall the app. Videos already saved to the gallery or shared, and any data backed up by Android system cloud backup, must be managed or deleted there.

Sharing with third parties. We do not collect, sell or share your data with anyone.

Children. Not directed to children under 13.

Changes. We will update this page and its date when processing changes.
